Ayiri Oladunmoye

Ayiri is an ex-banker, human resource expert, and founder of Oaken Events Limited, one of Africa's largest event companies. She has organized major social, corporate, and public events across and beyond Nigeria.

Since 2010, she has helped influence and reposition Nigeria's event industry, producing the first Events Directory, organizing one of the largest Events Fairs, and creating Atinuda, a major gathering of event businesses in Africa.

She has also been involved in community and humanitarian work and has received recognition for her contributions, including being honored among the 100 Most Influential People of African Descent under the United Nations International Decade for People of African Descent.

Ayiri holds a degree in Sociology and Anthropology from the University of Benin and a Human Resource Management certification from Cornell University.
